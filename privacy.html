Privacy Policy — Morgan AI Agent

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. About this application

Morgan AI Agent (“the App”) is a private, single-user personal assistant application. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer and has exactly one user: its owner. It is not a public service, not a commercial product, and is not offered to third parties.

2. What data the App accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the App accesses the owner's own Google Account data through Google's OAuth 2.0 service, limited strictly to the permissions the owner approves:

  • Google Calendar — to read and create the owner's own calendar events
  • Gmail — to read, search, and send email on the owner's behalf
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — to create, read, and edit the owner's own documents
  • Google Contacts — to look up the owner's own contacts

The App does not access any other user's data, and has no other users.

3. How the data is used

Data is used solely to carry out the tasks the owner asks for through their own private chat interface with the App. No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose other than serving the owner's direct requests.

4. Storage and retention

Data is processed and stored locally on the owner's own computer. Credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions. The owner may revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the App's access to their Google Account.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, published, or shared with any third party. The App does not transfer Google user data to any external server.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to morgan.wpfagent@gmail.com.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL.
