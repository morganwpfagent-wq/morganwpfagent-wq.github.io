Morgan AI Agent

A private, single-user personal AI assistant.

Morgan AI Agent is a personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It exists to help one person — its owner — with everyday personal admin: managing a calendar, handling email, and working with documents and spreadsheets.

It is not a public service, not a commercial product, and it has exactly one user. It is built for private, personal use only.

What it does

Morgan responds to its owner's requests through a private chat interface. Typical tasks include:

  • Reading, searching, and sending the owner's own email
  • Adding, reading, and managing entries on the owner's own calendar
  • Creating, reading, and editing the owner's own documents and spreadsheets

How it connects to Google

Morgan connects to its owner's own Google Account using Google's OAuth 2.0 service. Access is granted directly by the owner during sign-in, is limited to the specific Google permissions the owner approves, and can be revoked by the owner at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data handling

All data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. No Google user data is sold, shared, published, or transferred to any third party. The app's use of Google API data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
